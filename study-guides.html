Common study questions

How much should I study each day?

There is no single number that works for everyone. Start with a realistic block you can focus through, allow time for school and other subjects, and review the plan each week.

Should I revise before starting a new topic?

Review the prerequisites if they feel uncertain. A short recall session can help you spot gaps before you begin new material.

What should I do when I get a question wrong?

Identify whether the issue was a concept, strategy, calculation or missed condition. Write the correction in your own words, then revisit a similar question after a gap.

Preparing for JEE is a long-term process. Adapt these suggestions to your current syllabus, school commitments and the official information for your exam year.
