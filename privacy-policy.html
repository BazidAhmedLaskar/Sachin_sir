Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit this independent educational website. This site is intended for a general audience; please do not submit sensitive personal information through the contact form.

Information you provide

If you contact us, you provide your name, email address and message. We use these details to review and respond to your enquiry and to address corrections or feedback. The contact form is designed to be processed by Netlify, our hosting provider. Avoid sending passwords, financial information or other sensitive data.

Hosting and technical information

Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times and browser details to deliver, secure and operate the site. See Netlify's privacy notice for details of its practices.

Advertising and cookies

The home page loads Google’s AdSense script, and we may use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address when its script is requested. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads based on visits to this and other websites, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements. Google may also use data for non-personalised advertising and fraud prevention. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage ad personalisation through My Ad Center.

You can control or remove cookies through your browser settings. Where required, we will seek the relevant consent before using cookies or personal data for advertising. Availability of advertising and consent options depends on how the site is configured and where you are located.

Study toolkit storage

If you use the study toolkit, your entries and checklist choices are stored in your browser’s local storage on your device so that they are available when you return. They are not submitted to us by the toolkit. You can clear them using the toolkit’s clear button or your browser’s site-data settings. Avoid entering sensitive personal information.

Third-party media

The home page displays a lecture thumbnail hosted by YouTube. When your browser loads it, YouTube may receive technical information such as your IP address. If you follow the link, YouTube’s own privacy practices apply. See Google’s privacy policy.

External links

Links to other websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Questions and requests

To ask about this policy or request access to or deletion of information you submitted, use our contact form. We will consider requests under applicable law. We may update this policy when our practices change.
