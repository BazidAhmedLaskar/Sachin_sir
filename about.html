About this site

An independent profile and study resource for students exploring JEE mathematics.

What you will find here

This site brings together a profile of educator Sachin Jakhar and practical articles about planning, problem-solving and revision. The study guides are general educational material: they are designed to help students develop a process, not to promise a particular rank or result.
